# Career Expansion: Brand Deals, Luxury Flex Assets, Sample Clearance & Advanced Catalog Editions

This update expands the artist career simulation with four interconnected industry pillars: a **Brand Deals & Ambassadorships App**, a **Luxury Real Estate & Flex Assets App**, **Studio Sample Clearance & Copyright Legal Mechanics**, and full Studio/Release support for **Deluxe Editions, Commercial & Street Mixtapes, Anniversary/Remastered Re-releases, and Greatest Hits Compilations**.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The plan below incorporates all four preferences confirmed during our design review:

- **Confirmed Decision 1 — Dedicated Phone Apps**: Brand Ambassadorships and Luxury Real Estate & Flex Assets will launch as two distinct, full-screen apps on the phone's Apps launcher (**Brand Deals** and **Luxury Estates & Flex**), each with its own status badge and dedicated management experience.
- **Confirmed Decision 2 — Pre-Clearance + Copyright Lawsuit Risk**: When writing or producing a track in the Studio, players can interpolate or sample iconic records. They can either negotiate **Pre-Clearance** (upfront flat fee + publishing royalty split) or **Release Uncleared** at zero upfront cost—carrying a daily risk of a copyright infringement claim, DMCA freeze, or settlement negotiation if the record blows up.
- **Confirmed Decision 3 — All 4 Advanced Project Formats**: The Studio & Release workflow will support **Deluxe Edition upgrades** (attaching bonus tracks to an existing released album), **Commercial & Free Street Mixtapes** (lower production/distribution cost and massive street hype velocity), **Anniversary & Remastered Re-releases** of catalog projects, and **Compilation / Greatest Hits** albums.
- **Confirmed Decision 4 — Status Symbols, Hype Buffs & Daily Upkeep**: Mansions, penthouses, hypercars, private jets, watches, and custom chains act as pure status symbols that boost **Hype**, **Prestige**, and **Flex Score** while adding transparent daily maintenance/upkeep line items to the Daily Expenses breakdown.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**:
  1. **Brand Ambassadorships & Endorsements App**: Connects the artist (and optionally their superstar persona) with global fashion houses, streetwear labels, beverage giants, audio hardware makers, and luxury watchmakers. Contracts feature tier-gated eligibility (Popularity, Hype, Fans, and Flex Score), upfront signing bonuses, recurring daily/campaign payouts, campaign activations (Lookbook shoots, Met Gala/Fashion Week appearances, commercial spots), and exclusivity clauses.
  2. **Luxury Real Estate & Flex Assets App**: A curated high-net-worth catalog of Mansions & Penthouses, Hypercars, Private Aviation & Yachts, and Haute Horology & Custom Diamond Chains. Each asset increases the artist's **Flex & Prestige Rating**, grants permanent Hype floor/gain multipliers, triggers viral `@PopBase` / lifestyle X reactions upon purchase, and adds realistic daily upkeep (property tax, staff, insurance, hangar fees) to the daily expense ledger.
  3. **Sample Clearance & Copyright Legal Mechanics**: Lets artists weave classic soul, hip-hop, pop, or electronic samples into new songs for an immediate quality and viral hook boost. Players choose between clearing the sample upfront (negotiating flat fee vs. publishing percentage, aided by Legal Counsel if hired) or dropping the track uncleared. Uncleared hits can trigger **Copyright Infringement Notices** from publishing estates—forcing the player to settle out of court, surrender a heavier publishing cut, or fight in court.
  4. **Deluxe Editions, Mixtapes, Anniversary Re-releases & Greatest Hits**: Expands project creation and distribution beyond standard EPs and studio albums. Players can upgrade an existing released album into a **Deluxe Edition** (combining its streams/sales momentum with 2–6 new bonus tracks), drop **Free or Commercial Mixtapes** to ignite street credibility and core fan growth at low cost, issue **Remastered / Anniversary Editions** to revitalize older catalog eras, or curate **Greatest Hits Compilations** from their top-performing singles.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Music career simulation players who want deeper financial sinks for mid-to-late-game wealth, authentic music-business risk/reward decisions in the studio, and realistic catalog era extensions.
- **Key Value**: Gives players meaningful ways to spend millions of dollars on status and lifestyle, introduces high-stakes copyright strategy to songwriting, and mirrors real-world chart tactics like Deluxe boosts and mixtape runs.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows

1. **Signing & Activating a Brand Ambassadorship**:
   - Open **Apps → Brand Deals**.
   - Browse offers across 5 industry verticals (*Haute Couture & Fashion*, *Athletic & Streetwear*, *Tech & Audio*, *Beverage & Lifestyle*, *Haute Horology & Beauty*).
   - Inspect contract requirements (minimum Popularity, Hype, Flex Rating, and category exclusivity), upfront signing bonus, daily retainer, and required campaign deliverables.
   - Sign the contract to receive the signing bonus immediately and unlock the **Execute Campaign Deliverable** action (e.g., Global Billboard Shoot, Runway Front Row, Signature Sneaker Drop) for extra Hype and cash payouts.
2. **Acquiring Real Estate & Flex Assets**:
   - Open **Apps → Luxury & Flex**.
   - Switch between **Real Estate**, **Exotic Garage**, **Jets & Yachts**, and **Chains & Timepieces**, or inspect the **My Collection & Upkeep** portfolio view.
   - Purchase an asset (or liquidate/sell an owned asset if cutting overhead). Upon purchase, the artist's **Flex Score**, **Hype**, and **Daily Maintenance Overhead** update immediately, and major acquisitions generate social media buzz on X.
3. **Sampling & Legal Clearance in the Studio**:
   - When writing a song in the Studio, choose whether to **Interpolate / Sample a Record** (None, Underground Crate Dig, Iconic Genre Classic, or Global Megahit Interpolation).
   - If a sample is selected, open the **Sample Clearance Desk** before or during release:
     - **Option A — Clear Sample Now**: Pay the negotiated flat advance + agree to a publishing royalty split (`5%–35%` of track royalties, reduced if the player employs a Music Attorney on their Staff Team).
     - **Option B — Drop Uncleared (Street/Viral Gamble)**: Pay `$0` upfront and keep `100%` royalties initially. Mixtapes enjoy higher leniency, whereas commercial singles/albums with high daily streams risk triggering a **Copyright Claim Modal** where the estate demands an emergency settlement, retroactive royalty seizure (`40%–75%`), or temporary streaming freeze.
4. **Packaging Deluxe Editions, Mixtapes, Remasters & Compilations**:
   - Open **Studio → Create Project** (and Release Modal):
     - **Deluxe Edition**: Select an already-released Studio Album as the parent project, attach `2–6` newly recorded bonus tracks, customize the Deluxe cover/title suffix (e.g., `(Deluxe)`), and release to surge the album's weekly Billboard 200 units and streaming velocity.
     - **Mixtape (Free Street Tape or Commercial Mixtape)**: Select `5–18` tracks. Free Street Mixtapes waive distribution fees and supercharge Hype + Fan conversion; Commercial Mixtapes chart on Billboard 200 with reduced packaging overhead.
     - **Anniversary / Remastered Edition**: Select an older released project to remaster with upgraded audio fidelity (`Hi-Res Lossless / Dolby Atmos` boost) and bonus vault tracks, reigniting catalog daily streams.
     - **Greatest Hits / Compilation**: Bundle `8–20` previously released hit singles into a career retrospective compilation that generates steady pure sales and catalog streams.

### Visual Identity & Theme

- **Aesthetic Direction**: High-contrast, tactile mobile-first luxury & music-executive interface that respects the existing iOS/dark-mode design language while giving each new app a distinct editorial mood.
- **Color Palette & Mood**:
  - **Brand Deals App**: Editorial champagne gold (`#D4AF37`), obsidian (`#0B0C10`), and crisp alabaster typography for fashion/luxury house contracts.
  - **Luxury Estates & Flex App**: Deep emerald-noir (`#071310`) with brushed platinum and warm amber accents (`#F59E0B`) for real estate portfolios, exotic cars, and horology.
  - **Sample Clearance & Legal Desk**: Executive slate (`#0F141C`) with crimson risk indicators (`#F43F5E`) for uncleared copyright exposure and emerald (`#10B981`) for cleared publishing licenses.
- **Typography & Hierarchy**: Clean display headings paired with `font-mono tabular-nums` for all dollar payouts, royalty percentages, daily upkeep costs, and stream/unit counters. Metadata uses clean inline typographic separators (`·`) with zero pill clutter.
- **Interactive Feedback & Motion**: Instantaneous tab transitions, live royalty/upkeep preview calculators before signing or purchasing, and clear status indicators in the Daily Expenses breakdown.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1 — Separate Modular Engine & UI Files Per Feature**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Build each of the four requested systems in its own dedicated engine and UI module while connecting them cleanly to `useGame.ts`, `DailyExpensesEngine`, and `Studio`.
  - *Why*: Strictly honors the project's modular architecture rule to prevent regressions and keep each system self-contained and easy to maintain.
- **Decision 2 — Pure Status & Hype Buffs with Daily Upkeep for Flex Assets**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Flex Assets grant immediate Hype boosts, ongoing daily Hype retention/floor buffs, and a composite **Flex Score** (which also helps unlock Tier-1 Luxury Brand Ambassadorships), offset by daily maintenance costs in `dailyExpensesEngine.ts`.
  - *Why*: Matches your confirmed preference for pure status symbols and Hype boosts with daily maintenance costs, creating a realistic late-game money sink without turning the music sim into a landlord management game.
- **Decision 3 — Dual-Path Sample Clearance (Pre-Clearance vs. Uncleared Lawsuit Risk)**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Samples boost song quality (`+4` to `+12`) and initial viral hook potential. Pre-clearing locks in a fair upfront fee and modest royalty split; leaving a commercial track uncleared saves cash upfront but rolls a daily exposure check scaled by the song's daily streams.
  - *Why*: Captures the real-world tension of bedroom/mixtape producers versus major-label clearances and gives the existing Legal Counsel staff member a concrete mechanical synergy.

### Data Model & State

1. **Brand Ambassadorships (`BrandDealsState`)**:
   - Tracks `activeContracts` (brand ID, vertical, tier, signingBonus, dailyPayout, hypeBuff, remainingDays, deliverablesCompleted, exclusivityCategory), `availableOffers`, `completedHistory`, and `totalEndorsementEarnings`.
2. **Real Estate & Flex Assets (`LuxuryAssetsState`)**:
   - Tracks `ownedAssetIds`, `purchasedDates`, `totalAssetValue`, `totalDailyUpkeep`, `flexScore` (0–100), and `dailyHypeRetentionBuff`.
3. **Sample Clearance & Legal (`SampleClearanceInfo` on `Song` + `LegalDisputeState`)**:
   - Each song optionally stores `sampleInfo`: `{ sampleTier: 'none' | 'underground' | 'classic' | 'megahit', sampleTitle: string, originalArtist: string, clearanceStatus: 'none' | 'cleared' | 'uncleared' | 'disputed' | 'settled', upfrontCostPaid: number, publishingRoyaltyPct: number, qualityBoost: number }`.
   - Active copyright disputes are surfaced in a Legal Notice card/modal allowing immediate settlement, royalty renegotiation, or contesting in court.
4. **Deluxe, Mixtapes, Remasters & Compilations (`Project` extensions)**:
   - Extends `Project.type` to support `'single_pack' | 'ep' | 'album' | 'mixtape' | 'deluxe' | 'remaster' | 'compilation'`, plus `mixtapeModel?: 'free_street' | 'commercial'`, `parentProjectId?: number` (for Deluxe & Remastered editions), and `bonusTrackIds?: number[]`.

### Interactive Component & State Mapping

- **Brand Deals App (`activeApp === 'brands'`)**: Filter by vertical, inspect brand offer requirements (Popularity, Hype, Flex Score), sign contract (validating category exclusivity), trigger campaign shoot deliverables (with cooldown), and terminate contract early (with penalty).
- **Luxury Estates & Flex App (`activeApp === 'luxury'`)**: Filter by asset class (*Real Estate*, *Supercars*, *Aviation & Marine*, *Jewelry & Watches*), inspect purchase price, daily upkeep, and Hype/Flex boost, buy asset (deducting cash and posting X news for flagship purchases), or sell owned asset at `75%` resale value to reduce daily expenses.
- **Sample Clearance & Legal Desk**: Integrated directly into the Song Writing/Recording & Release flow plus a dedicated **Legal & Clearance Desk** modal in the Studio to pre-clear pending samples or resolve active copyright infringement claims on uncleared hits.
- **Advanced Project Creator & Release Flow**: Step-by-step project type selector in `EnhancedProjectCreatorModal` and `ActionModal` supporting parent album selection for **Deluxe** and **Anniversary Remasters**, **Free vs. Commercial Mixtape** toggle, and **Greatest Hits** single bundling.
